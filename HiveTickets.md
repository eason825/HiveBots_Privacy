# Privacy Policy

**Bot:** Team Hive Support Bot (Discord Application ID `1473827490597437440`)
**Last updated:** October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Team Hive Support Bot ("the Bot", "we", "us") accesses, how it is used, and how it is stored. The Bot is a private support-ticket bot built for and operated only within the **Team Hive** Discord server.

By being a member of the Team Hive server or interacting with the Bot, you agree to this policy.

---

## 1. Information the Bot Accesses

The Bot only accesses data that Discord provides through its API, and only for the features listed below.

### Server member information
- Your Discord user ID, username, and display name
- Your server roles
- Your avatar and role colour

**Why:** to create private ticket channels you can access, to check whether a user is allowed to run staff commands (Helper/Moderator roles only), and to display names and avatars in ticket transcripts.

### Message content
- The text of messages sent **inside ticket channels**
- Messages that start with the Bot's command prefix (`support!`)

**Why:** to generate a transcript of a support ticket when it is closed, and to respond to prefix commands.

The Bot does **not** read, log, or process message content in regular (non-ticket) channels beyond detecting its own commands.

### Information the Bot does NOT collect
- Presence or activity data (online status, games, Spotify, etc.)
- Voice data
- Email addresses, IP addresses, or payment information
- Direct messages you send to the Bot (it does not process DM replies)

---

## 2. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide the Bot's support features:

- **Ticket creation:** creating a private channel visible only to you and Team Hive staff, posting a ticket notice, and sending you a DM with a link to your ticket.
- **Ticket logging:** posting a short notice (your mention, user ID, and ticket reason) to a staff-only log channel when a ticket is opened.
- **Ticket transcripts:** when a ticket is closed, the text messages in that ticket are compiled into an HTML transcript and uploaded to a staff-only log channel, after which the ticket channel is deleted.
- **Permission checks:** confirming that only authorized staff can use staff commands.

Your data is **never** sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train machine learning or AI models.

---

## 3. Data Storage and Retention

- The Bot does **not** use a database and does **not** store your data on external servers or disks.
- Transcripts are generated in memory and uploaded directly to a staff-only channel **inside the Team Hive Discord server**. They remain there under Discord's own storage and are subject to [Discord's Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy).
- Transcripts stay in the log channel until removed by Team Hive staff.

---

## 4. Who Can See Your Data

- **Ticket channels:** only you, Team Hive Helpers, and Team Hive Moderators.
- **Transcripts and ticket logs:** only Team Hive staff with access to the staff log channel.
- **Discord:** all data is processed through Discord and is also subject to Discord's terms and policies.

---

## 5. Your Choices and Rights

- **Opting out:** the Bot only reads message content inside ticket channels. If you do not want your messages included in a transcript, do not send them in a ticket channel. Leaving the Team Hive server stops the Bot from accessing your member information.
- **Deletion requests:** you may ask Team Hive staff to delete transcripts or ticket logs that include your messages. Contact us using the details below and we will remove them from the log channel where reasonably possible.
- **Questions:** you may ask what data related to you exists in ticket logs.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

The Bot is intended only for users who meet Discord's minimum age requirement (13 or older, or higher where required by local law). We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under that age.

---

## 7. Security

Access to ticket channels and logs is restricted using Discord's role and channel permissions. The Bot's credentials are kept private and are not shared.

---

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Bot after changes means you accept the updated policy.
